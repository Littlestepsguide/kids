The research behind it

Built on Decades of Trusted Child-Development Research

Every activity in this guide is grounded in the work of three of the most influential minds in child development, translated out of academic language and into simple, playful exercises any parent can run at home in 10 minutes, no training required.

Maria Montessori

Physician & Educator

The Italian doctor whose hands-on method reshaped early education worldwide. Her core insight: children learn best through purposeful, independent activity, not lectures or screens.

Jean Piaget

Developmental Psychologist

The Swiss psychologist who mapped how children's thinking develops in distinct stages, proving that activities only work when they truly match a child's age and abilities.

Lev Vygotsky

Psychologist & Learning Theorist

The pioneer who showed that kids grow fastest when challenged just beyond their current level, with a little guided support, the exact principle behind this guide's step-by-step progression.
